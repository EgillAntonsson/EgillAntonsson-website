This is a blog post about Functional Programming (henceforth shortened to FP) in general, and not about a specific programming language.
I recommend reading the Functional Programming in C# (2nd edition) book by Enrico Buonanno, and the Functional Programming in Scala (2nd edition) book by Paul Chiusano and Rúnar Bjarnason (my grandfather and his grandfather were brothers).
These books are both good introduction to FP (but they they also go deep into FP design), and although focusing on C# and Scala respectively (both multi-paradigm languages / not FP only), they explain well the FP concepts and principles that are applicable to other languages as well.

FP emphasizes functions as first-class values while avoiding state mutation.
